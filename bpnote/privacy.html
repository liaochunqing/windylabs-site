Privacy Policy

BP Note · Effective date: July 28, 2026

BP Note puts your privacy first. Your blood pressure data belongs to you and stays under your control.

What we collect

  • Nothing is uploaded to us. We operate no servers and receive none of your data.
  • Your readings are stored locally on your device. If you enable iCloud Backup, they sync to your own iCloud private database — only you can access them.
  • The app requires no sign-up and collects no account, name, email, or location.
  • We use no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Apple Health

If you turn it on in Settings, BP Note can write new readings to Apple Health and import blood pressure records from the last 90 days. This data is exchanged only between the app and Apple Health on your device, under the permissions you grant in iOS.

Storage and deletion

All readings live in the app's local storage (and optionally your private iCloud database). You can delete any record by swiping it in the app, or erase everything by deleting the app.

Purchases

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment details.

Not medical advice

BP Note is a record-keeping tool. It does not measure blood pressure, and its classifications and statistics are for reference only — they do not constitute a medical diagnosis or treatment advice. Always follow your doctor's guidance.

Contact

Questions about this policy: windylabs@icloud.com

隐私政策

BP Note · 生效日期：2026 年 7 月 28 日

BP Note 把你的隐私放在第一位。你的血压数据属于你，并由你掌控。

我们收集什么

  • 不会上传给我们。我们不运营任何服务器，不会收到你的任何数据。
  • 记录保存在你的设备本机。开启 iCloud 备份后，数据只会同步到你自己的 iCloud 私有数据库——只有你能访问。
  • App 不需要注册，不收集账号、姓名、邮箱或位置信息。
  • 不使用任何第三方统计或广告 SDK。

Apple 健康

在设置中开启后，BP Note 可以把新记录写入 Apple 健康，并导入最近 90 天的血压记录。数据仅在 App 与设备上的 Apple 健康之间交换，范围以你在 iOS 中授予的权限为准。

存储与删除

全部记录保存在 App 的本地存储（以及可选的 iCloud 私有数据库）中。你可以在 App 内滑动删除任意记录，或直接删除 App 清除全部数据。

购买

订阅与买断购买由 Apple 通过 App Store 处理，我们不会接触或存储你的支付信息。

非医疗用途

BP Note 是一款记录工具，不具备测量血压的功能。其分级与统计仅供参考，不构成医疗诊断或治疗建议，请始终以医生的指导为准。
